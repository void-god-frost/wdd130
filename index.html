Nigeria, located on the Gulf of Guinea in West Africa, is often called the "Giant of Africa". With an estimated population exceeding 240 million people, it is Africa's most populous nation and home to one of the youngest, most vibrant populations on the globe.

The country boasts incredible cultural richness, shaped by over 250 ethnic groups—including the Hausa, Yoruba, and Igbo—and a sprawling landscape ranging from southern coastal mangroves to northern savanna. Economically, Nigeria stands as a major regional powerhouse driven by petroleum resources, a expanding tech sector, and an influential cultural export machine. Its entertainment scene, led by Afrobeats music and Nollywood cinema, shapes global pop culture. Known for its resourcefulness, entrepreneurial spirit, and deep resilience, Nigeria remains a vital economic and cultural anchor for the continent.
